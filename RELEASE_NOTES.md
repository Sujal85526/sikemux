# Sikemux v0.5.0-nightly.8

Your agent accounts move to Settings. Nightlies are signed and delivered exactly like stable releases, but they carry unreleased work and can break. Switch back to stable in Settings → About whenever you want; you keep the build you are on until a stable release passes it.

Everything below is new since v0.5.0-nightly.7.

## Accounts

- **Accounts are managed in Settings → Agents**, one section per provider: add an account, sign in and out, remove one, and choose whether chats move to the next account at a usage limit.
- **The rail only switches accounts.** Its account card picks the one new chats start on, and its menu ends with Manage accounts…, which opens Settings. An account that is signed out says so, and sends you to Settings to sign in.

For the complete patch history, compare [`v0.5.0-nightly.7...v0.5.0-nightly.8`](https://github.com/nodelike/sikemux/compare/v0.5.0-nightly.7...v0.5.0-nightly.8).
